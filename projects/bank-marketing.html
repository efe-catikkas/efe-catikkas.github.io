Bank Marketing Response Prioritization

A portfolio project built around a practical campaign decision: when contact capacity is limited, which records should be prioritized first? The workflow combines chronological validation, ranking-oriented model evaluation, capacity scenarios, segment profiling and decision-focused Power BI reporting.

BUSINESS QUESTION

Prioritize under limited contact capacity

Instead of treating the task as a simple binary classification problem, the model score is used to rank records relative to other records within the same campaign period.

The analysis asks whether a limited-capacity contact strategy can concentrate more positive responses into the subset selected for outreach.

APPROACH

Chronological and ranking-oriented

  • Preserve chronological campaign-period blocks.
  • Remove known target leakage such as call duration.
  • Compare Logistic Regression and Random Forest.
  • Select the model using Lift@20 on validation.
  • Evaluate once on untouched test blocks.
  • Export decision-ready outputs to Power BI.

INTERPRETATION

Important limitations

The project is retrospective. The model score is a relative prioritization score rather than a calibrated purchase probability, and the reported improvement is not a causal uplift or guaranteed campaign gain.
